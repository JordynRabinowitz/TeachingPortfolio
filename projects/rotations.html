Math 8 · Accessible assessment

Choice-based rotations warm-up

A short formative assessment designed to check visual understanding and symbolic rules for rotations about the origin without making memorization or language the main obstacle.

Math 8TransformationsStudent choiceBilingual supports
The context

For a unit on rigid transformations, I developed a start-of-class assessment that offers students a small amount of choice while still assessing the same core objective: recognize, perform, and explain rotations.

The design question

How can I learn whether a student understands a rotation conceptually, not just whether they have memorized a coordinate rule?

What I designed and contributed

  • Invited students to choose one visual problem and one coordinate-rule problem.
  • Included rotation rules as a reference so students could focus on interpretation and application.
  • Prepared English on one side and Spanish on the other to reduce unnecessary language barriers.
  • Asked for a brief explanation using “I know because…” and used student work to identify misconceptions.

Multiple representations help separate different sources of difficulty: seeing how a figure moves, interpreting clockwise versus counterclockwise, or using the corresponding coordinate rule.

Where this work stands
