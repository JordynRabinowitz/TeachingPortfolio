Interactive project · Classroom climate

BraveByDesign

A browser-based learning experience that invites users to navigate classroom scenarios, reflect on their responses, and think about building safe and brave learning spaces.

Web developmentInteractive storytellingClassroom climateGitHub Pages
The context

I developed BraveByDesign as a creative classroom-climate project, combining my interest in educational equity and student belonging with my background in front-end development. The experience uses classroom scenes and decision points to invite reflection.

The design question

How can an interactive website make questions about classroom climate concrete, participatory, and open to reflection?

What I designed and contributed

  • Built an interactive web experience around classroom scenarios and response choices.
  • Used a room-based visual flow and iterative interface design to make navigation approachable.
  • Connected the project to educational sources and reflected on the use of AI assistance during development.
  • Published an iteration through GitHub Pages.

Interactive experiences can invite learners to practice noticing, making a choice, and reflecting on the impact of that choice. The project also demonstrates how I connect creative coding to educational questions.

Where this work stands
