Math 8 · Lesson design

From tables to linear equations

A real-world lesson that connects rate of change, initial value, and equations while making every student’s thinking visible through whiteboards.

Math 8Linear relationshipsFormative assessmentStudent whiteboards
The context

I developed a Math 8 lesson connecting tables to equations. Students begin with a proportional pizza example, then examine game currency in a linear relationship with a starting value, and finish with a soccer-based rate comparison.

The design question

How can I see students’ reasoning during the lesson rather than waiting until their work is submitted?

What I designed and contributed

  • Established the repeatable routine Write → Show → Check → Fix before moving into the content.
  • Used pizza to model a proportional rate and game currency to introduce a nonzero initial value.
  • Paired individual whiteboard responses with teacher scans, feedback, and optional reteaching.
  • Designed a soccer exit task in which students compare rates shown in different representations and explain what slope means.

A correct number on its own does not tell me enough. The whiteboard process gives students a low-stakes way to show work and gives me immediate evidence for adjusting instruction.

Where this work stands

Prepared for a November 30, 2026 observation lesson. As of this portfolio draft, it is presented as planned instruction, not completed implementation.
